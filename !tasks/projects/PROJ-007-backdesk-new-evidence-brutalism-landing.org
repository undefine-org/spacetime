** Intent

New public site for backdesk.org, re-founded on the Djinn substrate vision
(~/code/ora/monorepo/docs/architecture/djinn-substrate-2026-07-22/000-intro.typ):
SMBs run on *processes that talk* — a guest books, a receptionist confirms, a
manager approves — and today that coordination lives in chat threads and heads
of staff. Backdesk = the substrate that turns declared processes into running
conversations, orchestration, and *evidence*.

Completely new branding (not the coral/Fraunces hospitality brand of
backdesk-v2). Built with CMS-flavoured Spacetime (PROJ-004 surfaces).

** Brand — "Evidence Brutalism"

- Thesis line: *Show, don't claim.* (vision doc "Truthfulness" conviction)
- Identity: the operations LEDGER — receipts, stamps, journals, audit hairlines.
- Palette: bone paper =#F2EEE5= · ink =#141715= · vermilion =#FF3B24= (THE
  accent, used like a rubber stamp) · ledger green =#1E6B4F= (verified) ·
  graphite =#6E7370=.
- Type: Archivo (Black/Expanded, display) · IBM Plex Mono (journal/telemetry) ·
  Inter (body).
- Moodboard: generated + approved in session (backdesk-moodboard.png).

** Copy doctrine (researched)

7-section landing formula (swiftcopy.io 2026 guide + unbounce): benefit-led
hero <10 words; problem/agitation in customer language; benefits not features;
3-step how-it-works; proof; FAQ = objection handling; one CTA repeated 2-3x
with risk reducer. Every section answers: do you understand me / can you help
me / can I trust you.

Customer: SMB owner-operator (hotel, clinic, agency) drowning in WhatsApp
threads + inboxes; not a developer; wants promises kept and proof of why.
