** Intent

Build a reusable, project-agnostic process that lets an agent interview a human
and derive brand + website copy from what was actually said — replacing the
default failure of AI-built sites, which is *fluent invention*.

Trigger: =projects/backdesk-new/= (v1) was built by an agent reading a technical
vision document and inferring every market/brand answer. The result is
internally coherent and externally ungrounded — =data/pains.json= contains three
quotes in quotation marks that nobody said, and =data/faq.json= answers five
objections nobody raised. v1's own honesty note says statistics were left out
"consistent with the brand rule: no claim we can't show" — correct about
numbers, but *quoted pain is a claim too*, and a fabricated customer sentence
is a worse violation than a fabricated percentage because it is disguised as
evidence.

** Key design decisions

- *Ericksonian split.* Utilization + pacing + indirection IN (documented
  practice, and the engine of Clean Language). Milton-model compliance patterns
  (presupposition, embedded command, yes-set, double bind) OUT of the
  interview: they are engineered to produce agreement, and agreement is the
  *failure signal* in Pass 1 — if the human keeps agreeing, the agent is
  talking. They are also B&G's reconstruction, not Erickson's validated method.
- *The completion mechanic's honesty gate.* "EXACTLY what I need" and horoscope
  copy use the SAME reader-completion machinery; the difference is whether the
  frame constrains admissible completions to the product's real affordance.
  G4 makes that testable rather than tasteful.
- *Concrete about STRUCTURE, open about INSTANCE* — the resolution of the
  specificity tradeoff. Explicitly labelled in 08-SOURCES as a defensible
  synthesis, NOT a validated named law (S26).
- *Situation over industry* (P7) — directly implements the user's instruction to
  get the hotel use-case out of the reflection.
