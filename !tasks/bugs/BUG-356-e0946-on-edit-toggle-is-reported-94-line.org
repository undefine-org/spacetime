NOTE [2026-08-10]: Fixed in d3d9dbfb. Root cause: `:root` and `*` were missing from the newline-led statement-start guard in `inline_args` (events grammar) and its CST twin — the list that already held `.class`, `#id`, `<el`, bare element selectors, `&ref` and `ident:`. A bodyless `@edit-toggle` therefore swallowed the selector as an argument and the rule's `{ … }` as its body, blaming E0946 on the directive.

The "94 lines above" and the non-monotonic bisect are both explained: the trigger was never line COUNT, it was whether a `:root`/`*` rule followed a terminator-less directive. That is also why it would not reproduce in isolation until the following rule was included.

Ordering was the subtle half: in `inline_args` the guard must precede the value-colon branch, which consumes any COLON unconditionally. Placed after it, only the `*` half works — measured exactly that way before correcting.

Gates in `tests/bug_216_silent_directive_drop_test.rs` (same family, not a parallel file): the target gate seen RED for both selectors, plus an over-widening companion proving `@data derive $area : $w * 3;` still parses.
