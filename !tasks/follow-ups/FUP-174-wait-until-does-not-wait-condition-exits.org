* Observed (2026-08-04, while gating BUG-274)
In a .test.st headless run, `@wait_until (window.__spacetimeData.brokenItems.error != null)`
and even `@wait_until (window.__spacetimeData.brokenItems.error)` exited
IMMEDIATELY while the polled value was still null (proven by a DBG probe
right after: {"data":null,"loading":true,"error":null}). The same value
settled correctly ~500ms later.

Expected: poll until truthy / throw "Timeout after Nms".
Actual: fell through on the first iteration, no timeout error.

Suspicion: the `%$condition` expr capture emits something mangled/truncated
(e.g. stops at the member-access head, which is a truthy object) — but not
proven; the while-loop emission itself could also be at fault. NB: no
.test.st in the repo uses @wait_until (zero call sites), so this may be
untested infra.

* Repro
tests/bugs/BUG-274-data-source-error-path-reference-error.test.st at the
BUG-274 fix commit: replace `@wait 500ms` with
`@wait_until (window.__spacetimeData.brokenItems.error)` and insert a probe
@eval after it — the probe sees error:null.

* Also observed (same session)
`@eval` does not accept multi-statement bodies ("expected R_PAREN, found
SEMICOLON") nor `async` IIFEs ("expected R_PAREN, found IDENT/FAT_ARROW") —
single non-async expressions only. Fine as a constraint, but undocumented in
docs/testing/GUIDE.md (the multi-statement workaround is an arrow IIFE).
