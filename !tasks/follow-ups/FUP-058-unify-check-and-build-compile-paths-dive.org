- `build` (src/compiler.rs `compile_pipeline` → `pipeline::compile_verbose`) threads the full pipeline: CompileContext with `import_scope`, the canonical-qualifier pass, and ALL `check_*` diagnostic passes (E0926 unbound qualifier, E0927 visibility, E0924 ambiguity, etc.).

EVIDENCE: `@scene/badge` with `@use "./badge.st"` → `build` reports E0926; `check` reports "✓ All passed". Same file, opposite verdict.

WHY IT MATTERS: `check` is the dev-facing fast validation command (editor integration, CI lint). Any new pipeline diagnostic must be MANUALLY re-wired into the CompileAnalysis path or it silently won't fire in `check`. Two parallel validation surfaces = drift by construction.
