* Parent: FEAT-137. Design: @research/spacetime-phoenix-liveview-bridge.org §1/§8.

Today server→client state flows only as (a) the one-time seed on connected
mount (push_view → st-set → SpacetimeLocal) and (b) {:reply} payloads to the
page's own fires. Server-INITIATED assign changes (PubSub, handle_info, another
user's action) do not stream.

* Fix
`@data subscribe $x from $lv : assign_name` (+ dotted sugar `$lv.count`):
the LiveView pushes changed-assign diffs (the reconciler's output, design §8
"diff data, never templates") and the bridge applies them via ST.set.
Unreferenced assigns are never wired (bandwidth win, design §9.2). Subscribe
targets ⊆ assigns is contract-checked (E0928's comparator grows a second rule).
