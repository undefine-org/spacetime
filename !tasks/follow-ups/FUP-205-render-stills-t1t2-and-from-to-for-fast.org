* Initial Message

Using whatever tools and techniques you want, make the absolute most impressive, most mind blowing, most visually stunning 30s promo video for Spacetime (this repo). I want something that looks like we paid a studio $5m to produce.

After that, I want you to do the same thing, but with Spacetime, and to compare/critique the two

Surfaced by FEAT-175. A 2fps preview of a 30s page took 66s per iteration (compile + Chrome + 40ms settle × 60 + software raster); 13 targeted stills would take ~5s. The studio capturer had this in 5 lines (scratch/promo-studio/render.mjs `--stills`).

Proposal: `--stills 1.5,9,24` writes PNGs — one `ST._clock.advance(t*1000)` per still from a fresh page load (advance is monotonic; sort the list and advance by deltas). `--from/--to` bounds the frame loop for a range render. Both are pure `src/render.rs` options; ffmpeg is not involved for stills. Also consider exposing `PAINT_SETTLE_MS` as `--settle` with the 40ms default (it is 72s of a 14min 1800-frame render).

Acceptance: `render demos/promo/ --stills 1.5,9 --out scratch/x/` writes two PNGs; tests/render/render_test.rs gains a stills case asserting the two differ in pixels.
