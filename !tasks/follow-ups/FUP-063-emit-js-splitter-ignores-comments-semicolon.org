* Context
During PLAN-041 W1, the FEAT-127 mcp-host primitive's `%emit js` contained a line comment with a `;` inside it (`// ...One bridge per region; no page-global singleton.`). This triggered a latent emitter bug.

* The bug
`find_semicolon_at_depth_zero` in src/emit/js_parser.rs tracks string literals and brace/paren depth to find statement boundaries, but does NOT track `//` line comments or `/* */` block comments. A `;` that appears INSIDE a comment is mis-read as a statement boundary: the chunk splits there and the tail loses its `//` prefix, surfacing the comment text as raw JS → SyntaxError at runtime. Same class as BUG-040 / BUG-041 / FUP-046 (lexer/parser edge cases in %emit js bodies).

* Why not fixed in PLAN-041 W1
A first-attempt fix (add comment-skip arms to find_semicolon_at_depth_zero) was applied by the W1 gap-fix agent but REGRESSED 3 template-definition snapshot tests — the new output showed `return element;;` (double semicolon), lost indentation, and broken `} else {` formatting. The comment-skip logic over-corrected and disrupted normal statement splitting. The fix was REVERTED; the trigger was sidestepped by removing the `;` from the mcp-host comment. The bug remains.

* What this FUP tracks
A correct comment-aware fix to `find_semicolon_at_depth_zero` (and any sibling splitters in src/emit/js_parser.rs) that:
  - skips `//` to end-of-line,
  - skips `/* */` to closing,
  - does NOT regress normal statement splitting (the 3 template snapshots must stay green),
  - is covered by a unit test: a `%emit js` body with a `;` inside `//` and inside `/* */` round-trips correctly.

* Acceptance
- New unit test: `;` inside `//` and `/* */` does not split.
- The 3 template-definition snapshots UNCHANGED (no regression).
- `cargo test --lib` green.

* Harmony note
When landed, the workaround comment in stdlib/__mcp__/primitives/host.st ("One bridge per region, no page-global singleton." — comma instead of semicolon) may be restored to its natural punctuation; not required, just possible.
