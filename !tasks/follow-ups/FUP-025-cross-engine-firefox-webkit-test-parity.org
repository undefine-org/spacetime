* Why
PLAN-027 W2 (FEAT-058) removed the Playwright npm-driver crate in favour of the
pure-Rust chromiumoxide CDP backend. chromiumoxide is Chromium-only, so the old
=spacetime test --browser firefox,chromium,webkit= multi-engine timing-parity
