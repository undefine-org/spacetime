Reference: https://github.com/dghez/mouse-effects-webgl-water/blob/main/src/components/water-distortion/ripple-effect.tsx

Implement continuous mouse-trail water distortion on undefine.org. The reference tracks mouse position every frame, computes velocity, and injects ripples along the path between previous and current mouse positions (8-point interpolation).

Key differences from click ripple:
- Continuous: runs every frame while mouse moves, not just on click events
- Trail interpolation: samples 8 points along prev->current mouse path to avoid gaps at fast movement
- Velocity-gated: ripple intensity scales with mouse speed (min(velocity * 10, 1.0))
- Content distortion: refracts underlying content through displacement map (not just specular overlay)
- Chromatic aberration: splits RGB channels based on displacement normal magnitude

Implementation should share the sim infrastructure with the click ripple primitive (same 3-buffer ping-pong, same wave equation). The display shader adds UV distortion of underlying content.

Requires: click ripple rewrite to 3-buffer float architecture first.
