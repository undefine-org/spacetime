W2.1 suite).

** W3.2 — `@on` head grammar: driver projection slot
Extend/replace on-event-dispatcher so the head accepts:
`@on <driver_expr> [<form-or-body>]`. Driver expr = `&name?.member` (with
optional args, e.g. `&.time($duration)`, `&viewport.scroll`) OR a `$signal`.
The member is looked up in the driver registry; unknown member → E-code
naming the registered drivers (NOT silent — the BUG-229/241 reporting rule:
the failure kind must be one the sink retains; a union capture per driver
member is NOT viable across an open registry, so this diagnostic likely
needs the registry read-back, following validate_form_refs' shape).
GATE: the article's `@on &.visible --rise;` fence compiles; `@on &.typo`
errors naming the registry.
