**Your root-cause read was exactly right**, and the generalization is worth keeping. The parser's "can I stop here?" check enumerated the tokens that may begin the next construct — and the list omitted bare identifiers, which is what a tag selector like `canvas` starts with.

Whenever you write that kind of check, the honest way to write it is to enumerate what may *follow*, and that list has to be exhaustive. A partial list doesn't reject the missing cases; it **eats** them. Rejection is loud and fixable. Eating is silent, which is what you experienced.
