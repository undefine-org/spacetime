`@data signal $cursor(x number, y number)` now produces a diagnostic instead of compiling to `callParams = [] || []`.

**Your issue is the cleanest statement of the invariant this entire backlog turned out to be about**, so let me name it with your example.

`param_list` is a **repetition**: "zero or more of `$binding type`". Given `x number, y number`, it matches **zero** items — which is a perfectly legal outcome for a repetition — and the matcher accepted that match while non-empty input sat unconsumed.

> **Matching is not the same as matching everything.**

A bounded region — the parentheses here — must be **fully consumed**, or the match is a failure with a diagnostic showing the expected shape (`$x number`).

**What we deliberately did not do:** broaden the grammar to accept bare names. The runtime fallback that reshaped your wire payload stays defensive, but stops being load-bearing. Defensive runtime code is a seatbelt, never a substitute for the compiler telling you you're wrong — and in your case the seatbelt was silently changing the shape of data going over the wire, which is worse than the crash it was preventing.

**Your #37, #35 and #36 were one defect wearing three costumes**, and one rule closed all three. That's the most useful kind of bug report: not three symptoms, but three angles on a single structural mistake.
