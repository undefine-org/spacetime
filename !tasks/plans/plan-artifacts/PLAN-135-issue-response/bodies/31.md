**Your root-cause list was complete**, and I'd underline two entries because both generalize well beyond this bug.

**The double-walk.** `UsageVisitor` walked `file.matches` *and* the scoped matches — but the former already contains the latter, so every usage was found twice. The tell is worth remembering: **when a result set is exactly duplicated, suspect a container being traversed through two paths** before you suspect the matching logic. Exact duplication is a structural signature; a matching bug produces messier symptoms.

**The span.** `@each` stored the whole `FormMatch.span` — the entire directive block — instead of the capture's own span, which is why your references covered the whole construct. Same root as #30: **the span you record is the span you can serve.**

Both are fixed as part of routing the editor through the compiler's live registry (#28), where spans are recorded properly at registration time rather than reconstructed afterwards.
