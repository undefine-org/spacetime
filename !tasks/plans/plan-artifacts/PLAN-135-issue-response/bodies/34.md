Warnings now render by default. `--quiet` suppresses them, `--deny-warnings` fails the build for CI, and warning-only runs still exit 0 unless deny mode is on — exactly the behavior you proposed.

**Why this was grouped with #26 and #27 rather than treated as cosmetic.** A summary line is a **contract**. Printing `✓ All 1 file(s) passed` over a file that has warnings teaches the reader that both the summary *and* the warnings are noise.

And once a tool has taught that lesson, **every future diagnostic you add inherits the discount.** You can write the most carefully-worded warning in the world; it lands on someone who has already learned that this tool's output doesn't mean anything. That's why it shipped in the same change as the two "diagnostics that never reach you" bugs — they're the same failure, and the honesty of the whole diagnostic surface is one property, not thirty independent ones.
