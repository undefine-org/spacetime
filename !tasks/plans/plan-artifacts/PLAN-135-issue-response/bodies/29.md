**Fixed — closing.**

Completion snippets are now generated **from** the real `%form` grammar, and there's a gate asserting that filled-in snippets actually compile — no `E0946`, no `E0910`.

**This was the highest-harm item in the LSP cluster**, for a reason worth naming: the editor was *generating* the exact malformed shapes that used to compile to nothing. Your issue and #25 were the same story told from both ends — the tool wrote `@scroll(name: ${1})`, the compiler shrugged and emitted nothing, and the author got a page that silently did nothing.

Both halves are now closed. Those shapes produce `E0946` (#25), *and* the editor stops producing them.

**The model.** A `%form` **is a grammar**: positional captures, sigils, literal words, a typed body. A snippet builder that models every form as "named parameters in parentheses" isn't approximating that grammar — it's discarding it and inventing a different one.

Once the LSP reads the real registry (#28), generating snippets from the grammar makes a non-compiling snippet **structurally impossible** rather than merely unlucky. That's the difference between fixing the snippets and fixing the snippet generator.
