* CORRECTION (2026-07-31) — the old "settled" block was a STALE DRAFT

This brief previously showed `@score(&.time(12s))` with `@scene "intro" { }`
blocks and `&cta: --fade-in(400ms), at 800ms`. That grammar appears NOWHERE in
SIP-001 or the launch article, and it violates three settled rules at once:

- parens on the head — `@on &.visible --reveal` has none, so `@score(...)`
  breaks the head pattern the two directives are supposed to share;
- `@scene` — collides with `@scene/camera`, path-qualified module references
  (docs/language/module-system.md:53), and string-keyed scenes reintroduce the
  string registry SIP-001 exists partly to delete;
- `&cta: --fade-in(400ms), at 800ms` — a colon and a comma before a trailing
  word, which is P4 (parens = the function's, trailing words = the language's)
  violated outright.

The real ground truth is SIP-001 §Proposal/4, unchanged since v4:
