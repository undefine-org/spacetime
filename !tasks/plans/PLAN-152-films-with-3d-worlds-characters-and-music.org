(The tweet demos — raea's showcase, PLAN-151: lit 3D scenes with shadows, a rigged character walking and falling, a

* Decisions (asked, answered)
- One 3D vocabulary lowered to BOTH engines: three.js (website, Chromium render) and raea's Bevy world through a
  page→world bridge patched into raea.
- Music as NOTES AS DATA in the score, synthesized deterministically, offline, no ML models.

* What was missing (verified)
- A score could animate only `@object` meshes; lights/models/particles could not be named.
- Camera forms only moved the canvas with CSS; =three-stage= never read the tracks (film.st.md §4 overclaimed).
- =three-stage= ran its own rAF + THREE.Clock: 3D ignored the render's virtual clock.
- No shadows, fog, glTF animation clips; no soundtrack reached a rendered mp4 on either engine.
- raea's HTML path never built a 3D world (pages always took the direct GpuView path).

* Design (as shipped)
- *The scene is data.* Every stage child is a PART: a kind + props in raea's 3D vocabulary (=Mesh= + material,
  =DirectionalLight=, =Model=, =Fog=, =Group=, =Bloom=, =PerspectiveCamera=), mirrored as hidden =<i data-3d
  data-props>= beside the canvas (=stdlib/3d/primitives/three-stage.st=: =ctx.part=). Score channels are a data table
  (CHANNELS); three.js realizers are a data table (REALIZE). =@object=, =@light=, =@gltf=, =@fog= and the new =@grid=
  are nameable; =&camera= is a part. The loop ticks through =ST._raf=.
- *raea bridge.* The runtime is told before it boots that there is no WebGL (=ST._stage3d.renderer = "none"=); raea
  reads each seek's =data-3d= tree, builds its scene rows over the film, draws them with Bevy and lays the page over
  them (=runtime/export/src/html3d.rs=). raea patches 8–11 in =stdlib/film/vendor/PROVENANCE.md=.
- *Sound as notes.* =@form instrument= (keyframes body: wave/pitch/gain/cutoff/…) and =@form pattern= (rows of
  =x . C2= per instrument, =$tempo=, =$step=) are two new form kinds (data in =form.st=); =@sound(--form [, notes:])
  [as &name] at <t> [for <d>];= is a score entry. =src/pipeline/score.rs= builds ONE synth program per score; the
  synthesizer is ONE first-party file, =stdlib/film/synth/synth.js= (=%vendor STSynth { source: local … }=), played
  live by =score-synth= and run in V8 by =src/render/soundtrack.rs= to write the soundtrack both engines mux.

* Follow-up DONE: the Chromium engine draws the same films (2026-11-06)
Request: "now please fix the Chromium part". Contact sheets of all three films on both engines agree frame by frame
(scratch/raea/films/par/).
- *The robot never loaded.* Chrome will not =fetch()= from =file://=, and the page was staged as a lone temp file, so
  =models/…= resolved under =/tmp=. =Stage= moved to =src/render/mod.rs= (shared by both engines) and Chromium now
  loads it over a loopback HTTP server (=Served=); the render waits for =ST._loading= (models), the page's fonts, and a
  driver that is not a stage's own loop (=tick._stStage=).
- *3D lagged the DOM by one seek* (a render's first still at rest; clips switched late). A signal's changes reach their
  dependents in microtask batches, so one =advance()= of hundreds of ticks delivered every write after the last tick.
  Fix at the root: a virtual tick settles its signal batch before the next (=ST._settleSignals= in =st.js=, called by
  =ST._clock=); a stage part folds when the score writes it, and draws once per settled frame. Gate
  =stdlib/3d/tests/stage-seek.test.st=, seen RED on e05fe34c.
- *Units.* The stage speaks three.js's units (the websites are tuned in them) and converts what raea reads: point/spot
  ÷ 8.95, ambient × 6.37, exposure → stops (=log2(E) − 2.26=, both ACES), emissive × the exposure gain; fog and a
  bloomed clear colour enter three.js divided by that gain. Derived from raea's =props.rs= constants and Bevy's
  exposure; the demo films were rewritten in these units (=exposure: 4.8=) so their raea look is unchanged.
- *Bloom* is a three.js composer (=Bloom= realizer), calibrated against raea on Disco; draws coalesce per settled
  frame (a bloom drawn every tick on SwiftShader timed out).
- *Fonts* ship with each film (OFL woff2 via =@font=); Chromium loads only what a page declares.
- Residual: a hard spot on a metallic surface (Disco at 15 s) reads hotter in three.js.
