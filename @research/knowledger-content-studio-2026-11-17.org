* Question
What would it mean to make Spacetime the center for making and managing a
brand's content (Your AI Auntie's brand brain / creative department, Claude
Academy's /on-brand, Claude Design, theaiimpact Brand Brain, creative-brain-
starter), with live editing of the files in the hierarchy? Answered by a
prototype: Ora-Ventures/knowledger-studio (private), served from
~/projects/knowledger-studio. Work log: [[file:../!tasks/plans/PLAN-160-typed-rows-and-live-data.org][PLAN-160]].

* What the market sells
- Your AI Auntie (Bemused Studio): a "brand brain" in Claude Projects
  (audience, positioning, voice, story, offers, funnel) and a creative-director
  skill with 24 plays, including a Brand Drift Detector and Series Continuity.
  Folders and markdown; no structure, no checks, no dependency between files.
- Claude Academy /on-brand: a skill rebuilt weekly from a guidelines folder,
  emitting a changelog. Claude Design: imported design system, live knobs,
  output checked against the system.
- theaiimpact Brand Brain: facts in the project, methods in skills, a scored
  Brand Guardian that proposes changes to source docs for approval.
- creative-brain-starter: a brain/ folder the agent writes lessons back into;
  every quote must trace to source; ads as experiments over five building
  blocks (UVP, persona, angle, format, style).

* What Spacetime adds
The source is the truth and every page is a view of it; one guarded writer
(rewrite) serves Studio, MCP agents and people; reflect/outline give every
value its file, span and type. So the brand brain becomes TYPED DATA with
DERIVED CONSEQUENCES rather than prose an assistant rereads:
- the claims register gates pieces (a HOLD claim blocks every piece resting on
  it; the publishable film cut is computed from it);
- the voice is a guardian (banned words, headline rules, channel limits,
  unsourced percentages, asset rights) re-run on every edit;
- usage is computed (which pieces use an asset, rest on a claim);
- every format of a piece is drawn from its row with the brand's forms, so a
  token edit restyles all previews.

(not yet built: see PLAN-160 Remaining).

* Moodboard (CARI)
174 references from 18 aesthetics of the Consumer Aesthetics Research
Institute, curated toward / partial / aside / away with what Knowledger takes
and refuses. Toward: Frasurbane, Austurbane, Eco-beige, Utopian Scholastic.
Away: Neoclassical Pomo (the columns-and-marble cliché of legal marketing),
Corporate Memphis, Corporate Synthwave, Silicon Dreams, Vector Minimalism,
Paperback Chic. Internal research only (CARI attribution guidelines).

* Content plan
113 pieces 2026-11-23 → 2027-02-26, 9 series, 4 campaigns (CMP-BAIL brand,
CMP-METHODES, CMP-MAITRISE factsheet, CMP-RELAIS gated on R1), 19 sourced
principles (LinkedIn B2B Institute 95-5, category entry points, Harry Dry's
copywriting rules, thought-leader ads). 16 pieces are blocked by HOLD claims
and open with R1/R2/R3. Validated against register.json programmatically.
