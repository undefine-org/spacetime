#+TITLE: Unified MCP — Design Language (organic interface birthplace)
#+CUSTOM_ID: mcp-design-language-organic-birthplace

* North star
This is NOT a dev tool. It is an *organic interface birthplace* — the warm,
living, generative studio where Spacetime interfaces are born. The live rendered
content (concern C3) is the organism under cultivation; everything else
(navigation C1, agent-comms C2, inspection, provenance) exists to NURTURE it and
RECEDES until needed.

* Principles
1. *The live layer is the stage.* The mounted content occupies the majority of the canvas, on a warm-glowing surface. Always the focal point. Chrome never competes with it for area.
2. *Chrome recedes into drawers.* Navigation, inspector, origin registry, comms are extensible BUTTON-DRAWERS — summoned by warm glyphs at the canvas edges, slide over/under the stage, dismiss when done. Never permanent columns.
3. *Warm, not technical.* Warm-dark palette (espresso + ember, never blue-black). No cold blues, no electric colors, no industrial grid. Flat with soft warm elevation. Editorial typography.
4. *Organic, not rectilinear.* Soft curves, large radii, gentle easings, warm glows. The interface breathes; negative space is deliberate.
5. *Extensible by composition.* Drawers are themselves Spacetime bundles (origin-tagged). A new drawer = mounting a bundle into a drawer-region. Same pipeline as content regions.

* Topology (reframed from the column grid)
- *Stage (C3)* — dominant center; live mounted content. ~70% of canvas. Warm-glowing surface.
- *Slim topbar* — wordmark + warm live ember + a few drawer-summon glyphs. Minimal.
- *Edge drawers* — left (environments / navigation), right (inspector + origin registry), bottom (agent comms / kit). Each: a slim summon glyph; expands to a warm panel overlaying the stage edge. Multiple drawers stack as a column of summon chips (the button-drawer pattern).
- *Comms ribbon* — when the agent emits affordances, a slim warm dock floats at the bottom edge; dismissible.

This REPLACES the 3-column + bottom-bar topology in =region-model-unified-mcp.org=
§"Region topology". The region MODEL (identity, lifecycle, isolation) is
unchanged; only the visual topology pivots from fixed columns to a dominant stage
+ receding drawers. Region ids remain: =stage= (was =main=), =nav-drawer=,
=inspector-drawer=, =origin-drawer=, =comms-drawer=. Drawer-regions carry
=data-st-drawer= in addition to =data-st-region=.

* Typography
- Humanist serif (Fraunces / Source Serif feel): wordmark, editorial headings, the live content's own identity/title.
- Inter: body, 14–15px, generous 1.6 line-height.
- ui-monospace: ids, paths, types, contract params — in warm taupe, not cold grey.

* Shape & motion
- Radii: 20px+ panels/drawers, pill buttons. Soft, organic.
- Shadows: warm =rgba(80,55,30,0.25)= diffuse; never grey/cold.
- Motion: drawers slide with soft warm ease. Live content mounts with a gentle fade/scale. Nothing snaps.
- Flat: minimal borders, no bevels, the only gradient is the stage's warm radial glow.

* Drawer model (extensible)
- A drawer IS a region: =data-st-region="drawer-{name}" data-st-drawer=.
- Summoned by a warm glyph (button-drawer chip) at the canvas edge.
- Multiple drawers stack as a column of summon chips; one open at a time, or pinnable.
- Drawers are bundles (origin-tagged): the workbench's built-in drawers are Stdlib; an env or agent can CONTRIBUTE a drawer (e.g. an agent sends a "review" drawer). Adding a drawer = mounting a bundle into a new drawer-region. One mechanism.

* Honesty ledger (what mocks depict that the runtime does NOT yet produce)
Every visual element below is a design target. Its prerequisite is a PLAN-039 epic, not a UI-only change:
- Live content mounted on the stage → M1 (region mount + bundle registration) — not built.
- Origin chips / origin registry rows → M5 (origin field on MacroRecord/FunctionRecord) — not built.
- Contract inspector param rows → OQ5 (FEAT-089 form-signature rail extended to templates) — not built.
- Per-region agent comms isolation → M4 (per-region instance identity) — not built.
- Warm-dark organic styling itself → a styling pass on the workbench chrome bundle; ships with the chrome.
