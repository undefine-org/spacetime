* Character vectors
Official inline Grok Bot marks extracted from https://x.ai/bot on 2026-11-14.
The original head and eye paths are separated into CSS masks in bots/ so color,
size, pose and eye motion remain independent. Full SVG marks are retained
alongside the masks for inspection. Original artwork rights remain with xAI.
The blue mask viewBox includes the entire official path, which extends beyond
the inline SVG's default viewport; the eyes share that same coordinate frame.
The red reverse-face mask is the official red silhouette; front and reverse
faces share one reusable 3D anatomy. The red front and mint body masks
use the filled Lucide heart motif (tag 0.468.0, ISC; icons/LICENSE) and a
six-lobe geometric mint mask reconstructed from the reference family; their eyes remain separate.
