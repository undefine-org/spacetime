Lucide official SVG assets, ISC license, https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide.
Most downloaded from main; smile.svg from tag 0.468.0. License in icons/.

* Character vectors
Official inline Grok Bot marks extracted from https://x.ai/bot on 2026-11-14.
The original head and eye paths are separated into CSS masks in bots/ so color,
size, pose and eye motion remain independent. No hand-drawn character geometry
is substituted. Full SVG marks are retained alongside the masks for inspection.
Original artwork rights remain with xAI. The final red and mint body masks
use the filled Lucide heart motif (tag 0.468.0, ISC; icons/LICENSE) and a
six-lobe geometric mint mask reconstructed from the reference family; their eyes remain separate.
